Why BloodLink?

Stop broadcasting. Start matching.

Traditional emergency blood requests often reach people who cannot help. BloodLink filters requests before notifying donors.

Nearby Donors

Find eligible donors within a configurable radius using their registered location.

Blood Compatibility

The matching engine filters donors based on the requested blood group before sending notifications.

Privacy First

Donor contact information remains hidden until the donor explicitly accepts the blood request.

Simple workflow

From request to connection.

BloodLink handles the filtering and notification process so people don't have to broadcast emergency requests to everyone.

01

Request

Enter the required blood group, hospital location, urgency and units.

02

Match

BloodLink checks compatibility, distance, availability and eligibility.

03

Notify

Only eligible nearby donors receive a notification for the request.

04

Accept

When a donor accepts, contact information can be revealed to the requester.
